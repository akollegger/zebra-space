A janitorial supply closet has five storage bays in a row, numbered 1 to 5, holding five items
one per bay: bleach, ammonia, vinegar, detergent, and sponges. The bays are open shelving with
no partitions, so air moves freely between neighboring bays.

1. The sponges are in bay 1.
2. The vinegar is in bay 3.
3. The detergent is not in bay 5.
4. The ammonia is not in bay 5.
5. Bleach and ammonia react to produce chloramine gas.

Where is each item?
