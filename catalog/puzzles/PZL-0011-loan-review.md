Priya and Sam are applying jointly for a mortgage.

General rules:

1. The couple's credit tier uses the lower of their two individual credit scores. If that score
   is below 600, the loan is Denied.
2. The couple's debt-to-income (DTI) ratio is their combined monthly debt divided by their
   combined monthly income. If that ratio exceeds 43%, the loan is Denied.
3. If not denied by rules 1-2, and the requested amount is within policy limits for their
   combined income, the loan is Approved.
4. If not denied by rules 1-2, but the requested amount exceeds policy limits, they receive a
   Counter-Offer.

This application:

5. Priya's credit score is 680; Sam's credit score is 750.
6. Their combined monthly income is $9,000; their combined monthly debt payments are $3,200.
7. Their requested loan amount exceeds the policy limit for their combined income.

What is the outcome of their application?
