Three houses stand in a row, numbered 1 to 3 from left to right. Each has a unique color —
Blue, Red, or Green — and is home to a unique animal — Dog, Cat, or Zebra.

1. The Cat lives in the Red House.
2. The Red House is the middle house.
3. The Blue House is directly to the left of the Red House.
4. The Dog lives in the Blue House.

Where does the Zebra live?
