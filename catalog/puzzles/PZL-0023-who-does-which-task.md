A four-person team must split four tasks — Editing, Design, Testing, and Docs — one task
each. Each person works at a different pace on each task; the table below gives the hours
each person would need:

| | Editing | Design | Testing | Docs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ana | 8 | 6 | 5 | 7 |
| Ben | 6 | 7 | 8 | 5 |
| Cara | 9 | 5 | 6 | 8 |
| Dev | 7 | 8 | 6 | 4 |

Assign one task to each person, with no task assigned twice, to minimize the team's total
hours.
