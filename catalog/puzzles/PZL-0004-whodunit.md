There has been a murder. The culprit is one of three suspects, using one of three weapons, in
one of three rooms.

Suspects: Miss Scarlett, Colonel Mustard, Professor Plum.
Weapons: Candlestick, Revolver, Rope.
Rooms: Kitchen, Library, Conservatory.

1. The culprit is not Colonel Mustard.
2. The culprit is not Miss Scarlett.
3. The murder weapon was not the Revolver.
4. The murder weapon was not the Rope.
5. The murder did not take place in the Kitchen.
6. The murder did not take place in the Library.

Whodunit — and with what, and where?
