A 4-by-4 chessboard has rows numbered 1 to 4, top to bottom, and columns numbered 1 to 4, left
to right. Place one queen in each row so that:

1. No two queens share a column.
2. No two queens share a diagonal.

The queen in row 1 is in column 2.

Which columns are the queens in rows 2, 3, and 4?
