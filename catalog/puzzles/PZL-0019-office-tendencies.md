Five colleagues — Amir, Bea, Chen, Dana, and Ehsan — share an office with five desks: the window
desk, the corner desk, the aisle desk, the door desk, and the center desk. Each colleague sits at
exactly one desk, and no desk is shared.

1. Amir usually sits closer to the window than Bea does.
2. Chen tends to take the corner desk.
3. Dana is often found at the door desk.
4. Ehsan, as a rule, prefers not to sit at the aisle desk.

Who sits at which desk?
