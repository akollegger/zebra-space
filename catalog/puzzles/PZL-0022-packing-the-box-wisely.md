A shipping box for a care package can hold at most 10 kg total. Available items, with weight
and how much the recipient would value each:

| Item | Weight | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Bag of rice | 4 kg | 5 |
| Hardcover book set | 3 kg | 4 |
| Cast-iron skillet | 5 kg | 8 |
| First-aid kit | 2 kg | 6 |
| Toolbox | 6 kg | 7 |

The first-aid kit must be included.

Which items should be packed to maximize total value without exceeding 10 kg?
