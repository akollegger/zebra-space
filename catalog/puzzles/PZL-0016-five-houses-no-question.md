Five neighbors live in a row of five houses, each painted a different color: red, blue, green,
yellow, and white. The chemist lives in the blue house. The painter lives three doors from the
chemist. The musician's house is between the red and green houses. The teacher does not live in
the end house. The florist lives next to the musician.
