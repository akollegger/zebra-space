A kitchen stocks three ingredients. Per serving:

| Ingredient | Protein | Iron | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Oats | 3 | 1 | 2 |
| Beans | 5 | 2 | 3 |
| Spinach | 1 | 4 | 4 |

Today's meal needs at least 20 units of protein and at least 10 units of iron. No more than 6
servings of any single ingredient may be used.

How many servings of each ingredient minimize total cost while meeting both requirements?
