Four countries lie side by side on a map: Avalon, Borealis, Cascadia, and Delmar. Some share a
border with each other:

1. Avalon and Borealis share a border.
2. Avalon and Cascadia share a border.
3. Borealis and Cascadia share a border.
4. Cascadia and Delmar share a border.
5. Borealis and Delmar share a border.

Avalon and Delmar do not share a border.

Each country is colored Red, Green, or Blue. Two countries that share a border must be colored
differently.

6. Avalon is colored Red.
7. Borealis is colored Green.

What color is Cascadia? What color is Delmar?
